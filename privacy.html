Privacy Policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

Short version: our checkup apps collect nothing and send nothing to us. For our in-person and remote services, we only use your information to do the job you asked for.

Arauza PC Security Checkup (Windows app)

  • The app reads security settings on your computer, such as antivirus status, Windows updates, drive encryption and firewall settings. It does not change any settings.
  • It does not read your documents, photos, email, browsing history or passwords.
  • It does not collect personal information, use analytics or tracking, or connect to the internet to send data. Results stay on your computer.
  • If you choose "Save report", the report is saved only where you choose. We never receive a copy.
  • Links in the app (such as "Open this setting" or arauzatech.com) open Windows Settings or your web browser only when you click them.
  • If you bought the app through the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles the purchase under its own privacy statement. We do not receive your payment details.

Arauza Phone Security Checkup (Android app)

  • The app reads security settings on your phone, such as screen lock, encryption, Android version and security patch date, USB debugging and Wi-Fi security. It does not change any settings.
  • It lists the apps that have Accessibility, device admin or notification access, so you can check you trust them. It does not read your messages, notifications, photos, contacts, location or passwords.
  • The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. It does not collect personal information or use analytics, ads or tracking.
  • If you tap "Share report", Android's share menu sends a plain-text summary only to the app or person you pick. We never receive a copy.
  • Google Play handles downloads under Google's own privacy policy.

Arauza Network Checkup (Windows app)

  • The app reads details about the network your computer is connected to: your Wi-Fi name and security type, nearby Wi-Fi network names, your router's address and which login services it answers on, your DNS settings, and the addresses of devices on your network. It does not change any settings on your computer or your router.
  • It never signs in to your router, never tries passwords, and does not test your other devices for weaknesses. To list devices, it sends one tiny message to each address on your local network so Windows can see which devices are there. It reads only the public details your router offers, such as its model name and the ports apps have opened through UPnP.
  • To check your DNS, it looks up two made-up website names through your own DNS provider. Nothing about you is included in those lookups.
  • On newer versions of Windows, the app asks for location access because Windows requires it before sharing Wi-Fi details. The app never reads, stores or sends your location.
  • If you mark devices as yours and name them, those names are saved only on your computer, in the app's own folder. Uninstalling the app removes them.
  • It does not collect personal information, use analytics or tracking, or send your results anywhere. Results stay on your computer.
  • If you choose "Save report", the report is saved in your Documents folder. We never receive a copy.
  • Buttons in the app open Windows Settings, your router's page or arauzatech.com in your web browser only when you click them.
  • If you get the app through the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles any purchase under its own privacy statement. We do not receive your payment details.

Arauza Cloud Security Checkup (Windows app, for Microsoft 365)

  • An admin of your organization signs in on Microsoft's own sign-in page in the web browser. The app never sees or stores the password.
  • With the read-only permissions the admin approves, the app reads your organization's Microsoft 365 security settings from Microsoft: sign-in and Conditional Access policies, admin roles, user and guest account names with their sign-in dates and two-step sign-in status, app consent and user settings, the SharePoint sharing setting and Microsoft Secure Score. It does not read email, files, chats or calendars, and it changes nothing.
  • The app talks only to Microsoft (login.microsoftonline.com and graph.microsoft.com). Nothing is sent to Arauza Tech Security, and the app does not use analytics or tracking.
  • The sign-in token is kept in memory only while the app is open and is not saved to disk. Results stay on your computer unless you choose "Save report", which saves the report only where you choose.
  • Your organization can remove the app's access at any time in the Microsoft Entra admin center under Enterprise applications.

Arauza Email Security Checkup (Windows app)

  • You type an email domain (for example yourcompany.com). The app looks up that domain's public DNS records (MX, SPF, DKIM, DMARC and MTA-STS) through your PC's normal internet connection. These are the same public records every mail server in the world reads.
  • No sign-in is needed, and the app never connects to your mailbox or reads any email.
  • Nothing is sent to Arauza Tech Security. The app uses no analytics or tracking, and results stay on your PC unless you choose to save a report.

Arauza Website Security Checkup (Windows app)

  • You type a website address. The app opens that website's home page and its /.well-known/security.txt file the same way a web browser does, and reads the secure connection details, the certificate and the response headers. It doesn't fill in forms, log in, or scan other pages or ports.
  • The website you check sees a normal visit from your computer, as it would from your web browser.
  • Nothing is sent to Arauza Tech Security. The app uses no analytics or tracking, and results stay on your PC unless you choose to save a report.

Arauza File Checker (Windows app)

  • The app checks only the files you choose, by dragging them in, picking them, or clicking "Check my Downloads". It reads each file to work out its real type, its fingerprint (SHA-256), any macros or hidden programs inside, its digital signature, and where Windows says it was downloaded from.
  • It asks Microsoft Defender, which is built into Windows, to scan the file in report-only mode. The app never opens, runs, moves, uploads or deletes your files.
  • The app makes no internet connections, uses no analytics or tracking, and keeps no history. Results are shown on screen and are not saved or sent anywhere.
  • "Check on VirusTotal" opens VirusTotal in your web browser with only the file's fingerprint, and only when you click it. The file itself is never uploaded. VirusTotal's own privacy policy applies to that page.

Security checkups and IT services

  • We only assess computers and accounts you have authorized in writing.
  • Checkup reports contain security settings and computer names, not the contents of your files or email.
  • Reports are shared only with the contact you name. We keep a copy for the period agreed in your authorization form, then delete it unless you ask us to keep it.
  • We never sell, rent or share your information with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

This website and contacting us

  • The booking form may be delivered through a form service (Formspree) or your own email app. This site does not use advertising cookies or trackers.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses for security.
