Questions people ask

Do the apps change my settings or collect my data?

No. Our apps only read settings and show you the results. They never change anything on your device, and your results stay on your device.

Is there a version for iPhone or Mac?

Not yet. Today our apps run on Windows 10 and 11 and on Android. iPhone owners can use our free iPhone security checklist.

We already have antivirus. Do we still need this?

Antivirus is one of the 43 checks in Arauza PC Security Checkup. Antivirus alone doesn't cover a stolen laptop, a reused password, a missing update or a risky setting. The app finds those.
