Privacy Policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

Short version: our PC and phone checkup apps collect nothing and send nothing. For our in-person and remote services, we only use your information to do the job you asked for.

Arauza PC Security Checkup (Windows app)

  • The app reads security settings on your computer, such as antivirus status, Windows updates, drive encryption and firewall settings. It does not change any settings.
  • To spot common risks it also looks at the names and versions of installed programs, the programs that start with Windows, the Windows hosts file, proxy settings and shared folders. It only checks whether automatic sign-in has a saved password; it never reads or shows the password itself.
  • It does not read your documents, photos, email, browsing history or passwords.
  • It does not collect personal information, use analytics or tracking, or connect to the internet to send data. Results stay on your computer.
  • If you choose "Save report", the report is saved only where you choose. We never receive a copy.
  • Links in the app (such as "Open this setting" or arauzatech.com) open Windows Settings or your web browser only when you click them.
  • If you bought the app through the Microsoft Store, Microsoft handles the purchase under its own privacy statement. We do not receive your payment details.

Arauza Phone Security Checkup (Android app)

  • The app reads security settings on your phone, such as screen lock, encryption, Android version and security patch date, USB debugging and Wi-Fi security. It does not change any settings.
  • It lists the apps that have Accessibility, device admin or notification access, so you can check you trust them. It does not read your messages, notifications, photos, contacts, location or passwords.
  • The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. It does not collect personal information or use analytics, ads or tracking.
  • If you tap "Share report", Android's share menu sends a plain-text summary only to the app or person you pick. We never receive a copy.
  • If you bought the app through Google Play, Google handles the purchase under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your payment details.

Security checkups and IT services

  • We only assess computers and accounts you have authorized in writing.
  • Checkup reports contain security settings and computer names, not the contents of your files or email.
  • Reports are shared only with the contact you name. We keep a copy for the period agreed in your authorization form, then delete it unless you ask us to keep it.
  • We never sell, rent or share your information with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

This website and contacting us

  • If you send a booking request or email, we receive your name, business name, email, phone (if given) and message, and use them only to reply and schedule your service.
  • The booking form may be delivered through a form service (Formspree) or your own email app. This site does not use advertising cookies or trackers.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses for security.
